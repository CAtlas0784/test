## Star Bitmask Bug Discovery & Protocol Synchronization (2026-09-05 Night Session)
1. **Challenge Bitmask Resolution (`star: 7`)**:
   - Discovered why Forgotten Hall floor 1 was stuck at 2 stars and MoC at 0 stars: `Challenge.star` in Star Rail protocol is a 3-bit mask (`1` = star 1, `2` = star 2, `4` = star 3).
   - Setting `star: 3` (`0b011`) explicitly told the client that only 2 stars were achieved.
